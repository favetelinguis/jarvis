# Page review checklist

Answer each item for one page with **met**, **not met** (say where), or
**not checked** (say why). Check in a browser at 1280 and at 320 CSS pixels
wide, with the keyboard only, in every color theme the board offers.

## Keyboard

- K1. Tab reaches every link, button, and control, in reading order.
- K2. Enter or Space activates every button; arrows move within radio groups.
- K3. Focus can leave every widget, dialog, and diagram with Tab or Escape.
- K4. The skip link is the first stop and moves focus to the main content.

## Focus

- C1. The focus indicator is visible on every stop, in every theme.
- C2. No sticky or fixed element covers the focused element.
- C3. After an action (send, move, close) focus lands somewhere sensible, not at the top of the page.

## Forms

- M1. Every control has a visible label tied to it.
- M2. Errors name the field and say how to fix it.
- M3. Required fields are marked in text, not only with color or an asterisk.

## Motion

- R1. With reduced motion on, nothing animates, slides, or pulses.
- R2. Nothing moves on its own for more than five seconds without a way to stop it.

## Typography

- T1. The page has one `h1`, and headings go down one level at a time.
- T2. Running text has lines under 80 characters.
- T3. Text can be zoomed to 200% without loss of content or function.

## Color scheme

- S1. Every text color meets 4.5:1 on its background (3:1 for large text).
- S2. Every status is also said in words or with a shape.
- S3. Buttons and inputs are distinguishable from their surroundings at 3:1.

## Responsiveness

- W1. At 320px the page does not scroll sideways; only diagrams and code scroll in their own frame.
- W2. Every target is at least 24×24 CSS pixels.
- W3. Nothing is cut off or overlaps at 320px or at 1280px.
